Product Owner & Business Developer

Problem
An agency selling events, banners, billboards, signage and exhibition booths needed a team and a repeatable way to turn client requests into briefs, quotations and finished work.
Discovery
Mapped how a request moves from client to quotation to designer to delivery. Ran market and competitor research to see where direct competitors in Egypt are strong and where they are weak.

Outcome
A hired and working team across design, 3D, sales and social media. A CRM specified from user scenarios through to the data model, with the backend planned in Node.js and MySQL. Client work now runs from brief to quotation in a consistent format.
